I'm a first-year PhD student in Physics at the **University of Texas at Austin**, where I work on theoretical cosmology and high-energy astrophysics. I completed my B.S. in Physics and Mathematics (GPA: 3.979) at **Southern Methodist University** in May 2026.

My research sits at the intersection of observational cosmology, dark energy, and early-universe physics. I've worked on constraining early dark energy models using Big Bang Nucleosynthesis, probing late-time dark energy dynamics through cosmological distance measurements, and building machine learning classifiers to identify high-redshift gamma-ray bursts as cosmological probes.

I am an **NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (GRFP) Fellow** and a **2025 Barry Goldwater Scholar**. I was also inducted into **Phi Beta Kappa** as a junior — one of 15 selected at SMU — and received the SMU Physics Robert S. Hyer Award.

## Research Interests

- Dark energy dynamics (early and late-time)
- Big Bang Nucleosynthesis as a cosmological probe
- Gamma-ray bursts as standard candles
- Machine learning methods in astrophysics
